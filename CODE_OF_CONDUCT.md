# Code of Conduct

JobPulse adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion focused on
the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues,
pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately via
[GitHub](https://github.com/Sthitadhi1). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer
may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
